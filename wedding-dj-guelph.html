Weddings in Guelph

Guelph couples tend to care about the details, which makes my job better.

Guelph has a particular character in the weddings I do here. There is usually a real interest in how the night is actually going to run, rather than a request for a list of songs. People ask about the timeline. They ask what happens between dinner and the first dance. Those are exactly the right questions, and they are the questions most DJs cannot answer because they were never involved in the planning.

I am, because I am also your MC. Before the day we build a timeline together in a shared portal, agree the running order, sort out who is speaking and how long they actually have, and align with your venue and photographer. On the day I run it from the microphone. Nothing is relayed, nothing is guessed, and you are not the one holding it together.

Guelph is around forty minutes from my office in Burlington, out the 401 and up the Hanlon. I am upfront about that rather than pretending to be local. What it means practically is that I plan my travel with a real margin and I am set up long before your guests arrive. I work the surrounding countryside as well as the city itself.

What I cover in a Guelph wedding day

  • Ceremony sound. A separate system with wireless mics for your officiant and readers, so nothing gets torn down mid-day.
  • Cocktail and dinner. The hour most receptions go flat. I treat it as part of the show, not a gap to fill.
  • MC hosting. Introductions, speeches, cake, dances, all led by someone who knows your family names before the day.
  • Reception DJ. A floor that stays full to the last song, read live off your actual room.
  • Uplighting and planning portal. Room lighting matched to your colours, plus a shared timeline you can edit any time.

The unglamorous part

Load-in, parking, curfews.

Nobody books a DJ for logistics. But the reason a night runs smoothly is almost always something nobody in the room ever noticed.

Drive time
About forty minutes from Burlington, out the 401 and up the Hanlon. I do not pretend to be a Guelph local. What I do is build a real margin into my travel and arrive early enough that setup is finished well before anyone sees me.
Load-in and setup
Farm and barn venues are the ones to plan for out here. Power is wherever the property actually has it rather than where the ceremony is, access can be gravel or grass, and gear often crosses open ground. Campus and heritage venues bring the opposite problem, with controlled access and parking permits. I confirm all of it ahead rather than discovering it on arrival.
Curfews and sound limits
Campus properties and venues near residential streets generally finish earlier than hotel ballrooms, and university venues can have their own sound policies on top of the city's. Your venue sets the limit and I plan the last hour to land inside it.

Can you do a barn or farm wedding near Guelph?

Yes, and it is a lot of what I do out here. I have worked Barn Swallow Fields at Thatcher Farms and CJ Country Events many times.
